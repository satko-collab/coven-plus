## Intellectual provenance

COVEN+ is an original decision architecture developed by Piotr Kowzan. It combines:

- a structured task contract;
- a short Meta-Reading layer;
- role-differentiated expert simulation;
- independent first passes;
- cross-examination and revision;
- preservation of minority positions;
- comparison with a direct-answer baseline;
- a public p/a quality audit.

The decision architecture was inspired by Long et al.’s multi-expert prompting method. Their experiments report benefits from simulating and aggregating several experts, but these results do not establish that voices generated by one model constitute independent evidence.

The methodological caution is informed by Smit et al., who found that multi-agent debate did not reliably outperform simpler prompting strategies without careful tuning. COVEN+ therefore requires a direct-answer benchmark and a stop rule when orchestration adds length without decision value.

The Meta-Reading layer is adapted from a meta-prompt presented by Nate B. Jones on the YouTube channel *AI News & Strategy Daily* in the video *ChatGPT-5 Prompting Is Too Hard: This Video Makes It Easy for You*.
